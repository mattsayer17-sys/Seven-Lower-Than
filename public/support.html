Help

Last updated 24 November 2026

How do I play?

Open the game and the first hand is already dealt. The welcome card shows the house rules; tap Rules for the full list, or replay the tour from the menu.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Online, make a table and share the link or code. Friends tap the link and sit down, no sign-up needed.

Someone is being abusive

Press and hold their name at the table to block or report them. Blocked players' reactions are hidden from you.

How do I delete my data?

Open your profile and tap Delete my data at the bottom. See the privacy policy.

Something is broken

Email us with what happened, your phone model and, if you can, a screenshot.
