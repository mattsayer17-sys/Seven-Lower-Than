Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Shiphead is a card game. We collect as little as we can, and nothing at all if you only play against the computer.

Playing on your own

Your profiles, records, achievements and settings are saved only on your device. We never see them.

Playing online

When you first go online we create an anonymous account for you. No email, phone number or password is needed. We store:

  • a random account ID and the name and avatar you pick
  • the tables you join, the moves in those games and where you finished, so games work and head to head records match on every phone
  • quick reactions you send at a table, kept for 7 days
  • people you block and anything you report
  • your network address, used only to stop abuse (request counts kept for 24 hours)

We use this only to run the game and keep it safe. We do not sell it, and there is no advertising or tracking in the game today. If that changes we will update this page and ask you first where the law requires it.

Who processes it

Game data is stored with Supabase in London (UK). The website and app files are served by Vercel. Both act only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Unused anonymous accounts that never went online are removed after 7 days. Finished games are kept for head to head records until you delete your account.

Your rights

You can delete everything at any time: open your profile and tap Delete my data. This removes your online account and all data on the device straight away. You can also email us to see, correct or delete your data. Under UK GDPR you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Shiphead is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Email hello@playshiphead.com. We reply within 5 working days.
